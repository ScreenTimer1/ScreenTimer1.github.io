Miqāt for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Miqāt is built to calculate and present prayer information without creating an account or sending your worship, location, or settings to a Miqāt server.

Effective date: September 9, 2026 · App identifier: com.jirryjirry.miqaat

1. Scope and developer

This policy explains how the Miqāt iPhone app and its Widget extension handle information. Miqāt is provided by Shenzhen Jirry Jirry Technology Co., Ltd (“Miqāt,” “we,” or “us”). Contact details appear in Section 12.

2. Data Miqāt does not collect

Miqāt has no developer-operated account system or backend. The app does not transmit your location, prayer settings, reminder choices, mosque information, or usage activity to us or to a Miqāt server.

The current version contains no advertising, advertising identifier, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, third-party crash-reporting SDK, remote configuration, cloud sync, or push-notification server. We do not sell personal information.

“Not collected” means information is not transmitted off your device in a way that lets Miqāt or a third-party partner access it. Information may still be stored locally by the app and processed by Apple system services as described below.

3. Information stored or processed locally

App settings

Miqāt stores your onboarding state, language, numeral and time format, appearance, selected city or location mode, calculation method, Asr and high-latitude rules, manual time adjustments, reminder choices, Hijri correction, privacy display choices, and optional user-entered mosque information in the app’s private local storage.

Optional location snapshot

If you choose Use Current Location, Miqāt asks iOS for When In Use access and accepts one fresh reading while the app is in the foreground. It stores the latest coordinate, device time zone at capture, capture time, horizontal accuracy, and whether iOS supplied full or reduced accuracy.

These values are used locally for prayer times, Qibla, calendars, reminders, and widgets. Miqāt does not request background location, maintain a location history, or transmit location to the developer or a server. Refreshing replaces the previous snapshot. Choosing a manual city removes the saved location snapshot. You can use the app with a manual city and without Location permission.

Compass

While the Qibla screen is visible and the app is active, Miqāt may read the device heading to show compass guidance and sensor quality. Heading readings are held in memory only and are not saved as a direction history or transmitted to us.

4. Widget and App Group

Full app settings, reminder rules, alarm identifiers, and optional mosque information remain in the main app’s private local storage.

To let the Widget calculate offline, Miqāt shares a limited snapshot with its Widget through the Apple App Group group.com.jirryjirry.miqaat. That snapshot can include the resolved coordinate, time zone and display name, location mode and capture time, calculation and display choices, privacy flags, configuration revision, configured state, and a prayer-time projection.

The Widget does not receive reminder rules, alarm identifiers, mosque information, or the complete app settings, and it does not request Location access itself. Widget content can appear on system surfaces. Miqāt offers settings to hide the city on the Home Screen Widget and the prayer name on supported Lock Screen surfaces.

5. Notifications and Prayer Alarms

Standard local notifications

Standard reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. Miqāt does not use a push-notification server. Unless the relevant privacy option is enabled, a notification may show a prayer name and time on the Lock Screen, Notification Center, StandBy, or a paired device. Focus, Silent Mode, notification settings, power state, and system limits may affect standard notifications.

Prayer Alarm on supported iOS versions

On iOS 26 or later, Prayer Alarm is an optional AlarmKit feature with separate authorization. AlarmKit alarms are more prominent and may sound through Focus or Silent Mode. If AlarmKit is unavailable, denied, or unable to schedule an alarm, Miqāt uses a standard local notification when notification permission allows. No alert technology can guarantee delivery under every device or system condition.

6. Your choices and permissions

  • Location: deny or revoke access in iOS Settings and choose a manual city. Revoking permission prevents future readings but does not itself erase an existing snapshot.
  • Notifications: change permission, sound, and Lock Screen presentation in iOS Settings; change individual reminder rules inside Miqāt.
  • Prayer Alarm: change Alarm authorization in the system controls available on supported iOS versions.
  • Lock Screen and Widget privacy: use Miqāt settings to reduce visible prayer or city information on supported surfaces.
  • Local data: use Settings → Privacy & Sources → Reset All Local Settings.

Reset does not revoke permissions managed by iOS. You must change those permissions separately in iOS Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

The latest location snapshot remains until it is refreshed, replaced by a manual city, reset, or removed with the app. Other settings and the Widget snapshot remain until changed, replaced, reset, or removed. Expired Widget projections are not displayed but may remain locally until replaced or reset.

Reset All Local Settings deletes Miqāt’s stored preferences, saved location, reminder manifest, tracked alarm identifiers, mosque information, and Widget App Group data. It removes Miqāt-owned standard notification requests and asks iOS to cancel tracked Prayer Alarms. Deleting the app removes its application data according to iOS behavior. Miqāt has no account and no server-side copy of your in-app location, settings, or mosque data. Support communications are described in Section 9.

8. Apple services, backups, and external links

Miqāt itself does not automatically make network requests. iOS services such as Core Location, local notifications, AlarmKit, device backup or migration, App Store distribution, TestFlight, and optional Apple diagnostics are provided by Apple and may process information under your device settings and Apple’s own privacy terms.

The app includes user-initiated links to engineering references hosted on GitHub and may link to this privacy or support site or your email app. If you open an external link, your browser, mail provider, Apple, and the destination service may process information under their own terms. Miqāt does not automatically attach your location, settings, or diagnostics to a support email.

These static privacy and support pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may receive ordinary request data, such as an IP address, browser user agent, requested page, and request time, to deliver and secure the site under its own privacy terms. We do not add analytics or advertising tools that use this information for tracking or profiling.

9. Support communications

If you contact support, we receive only the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, protect the service, and meet legal obligations—not for advertising or user profiling.

Support communications are retained for up to 12 months after the last correspondence unless a longer period is required for security, dispute resolution, or law. You may request deletion by contacting us, subject to any legal retention requirement. Your email or mail provider processes the communication under its own policy.

10. Children

Miqāt is a general-audience utility and is not offered in Apple’s Kids Category. It does not ask for a birth date or automatically transmit personal information to us. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent personal information through a separate support communication, contact us to request deletion.

11. Security and policy changes

Miqāt relies on iOS application sandboxing and an App Group shared only by the signed main app and Widget extension. No method of local storage is completely risk-free, so keep your device and passcode secure.

We may update this policy when app behavior, law, or platform requirements change. The updated page will show a new effective date. If a future version begins collecting data or adds a server or third-party SDK, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before or with that release.

لقطة الموقع الاختيارية

إذا اخترت «استخدام الموقع الحالي»، يطلب مِيقَات من iOS إذن «أثناء استخدام التطبيق»، ويقبل قراءة حديثة واحدة بينما يكون التطبيق في الواجهة. ويحفظ أحدث إحداثيات، والمنطقة الزمنية للجهاز عند أخذ اللقطة، ووقت أخذها، والدقة الأفقية، وما إذا كان iOS قد وفّر الموقع الدقيق أو التقريبي.

تُستخدم هذه القيم محليًا لحساب المواقيت والقبلة والتقويمات والتذكيرات والأدوات المصغّرة. لا يطلب مِيقَات الموقع في الخلفية، ولا ينشئ سجلًا للموقع، ولا يرسل الموقع إلى المطوّر أو إلى خادم. تستبدل إعادة الالتقاط اللقطة السابقة، ويؤدي اختيار مدينة يدويًا إلى حذف لقطة الموقع المحفوظة. ويمكنك استخدام التطبيق باختيار مدينة يدويًا من دون إذن الموقع.

البوصلة

عندما تكون صفحة القبلة ظاهرة والتطبيق نشطًا، قد يقرأ مِيقَات اتجاه الجهاز لعرض إرشادات البوصلة وجودة المستشعر. تبقى القراءات في الذاكرة فقط، ولا تُحفظ كسجل اتجاه ولا تُرسل إلينا.

٥. الإشعارات ومنبّهات الصلاة

الإشعارات المحلية العادية

تُجدول التذكيرات العادية محليًا عبر iOS، ولا يستخدم مِيقَات خادمًا للإشعارات الفورية. ما لم تفعّل خيار الخصوصية المناسب، قد يظهر اسم الصلاة ووقتها على شاشة القفل أو مركز الإشعارات أو نمط الاستعداد أو جهاز مقترن. وقد تؤثر إعدادات التركيز ونمط الصامت والإشعارات والطاقة وحدود النظام في الإشعارات العادية.

منبّه الصلاة في إصدارات iOS المدعومة

في iOS 26 أو أحدث، يكون منبّه الصلاة ميزة اختيارية تعتمد على AlarmKit وتتطلب إذنًا مستقلًا من النظام. تكون منبّهات AlarmKit أوضح، وقد تصدر صوتًا مع تشغيل نمط التركيز أو نمط الصامت. إذا لم تتوفر AlarmKit أو رُفض الإذن أو تعذرت الجدولة، يستخدم مِيقَات إشعارًا محليًا عاديًا عندما يسمح إذن الإشعارات بذلك. ولا يمكن لأي تقنية تنبيه ضمان الظهور في جميع أحوال الجهاز أو النظام.
