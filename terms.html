What Lesscreen does

Lesscreen helps you manage your own screen time through focus sessions, scheduled blocking and shared daily limits. It requires a compatible iPhone, iOS 16 or later and Screen Time authorization. It is not an emergency service, medical treatment or a remote parental-monitoring service. No account is required.

03

How blocking works

You choose the apps, categories, websites, times and days to restrict. A daily budget is shared across that plan’s selection, not granted separately to every app. On iOS 17.4 and later, earlier use from the same day may count toward the budget. Overlapping plans can continue to block an app until all applicable restrictions have ended.

Protecting a plan prevents stopping, editing or deleting it inside Lesscreen during an active blocking period. It does not prevent revoking permission in iOS Settings, nor make the app impossible to bypass. Apple controls the underlying system APIs, so operation can be affected by permissions, system versions and device conditions.
